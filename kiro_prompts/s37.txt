Goal. Vampire Fees finds a recurring bank fee, for example an account maintenance fee of 6,50 a month (yearly_cost 78.00 in the alert data). It cannot say whether that is a lot. public-fees adds one official public source: the payment account fee comparator of the Banco de España. The vf.recurring_fee alert then says, in one short sentence, how its yearly cost compares with the fees the Spanish banks declared to the Banco de España for the same service and account type, for example: of 63 payroll accounts declared on 23-11-2026, 41 charge 0,00 a year for account maintenance and the highest charges 240,00 (synthetic numbers). Decoration only, like the CPI: no new detector, no threshold, severity, title or source_refs change, the demo alert counts stay the same, and the data never decides whether an alert fires.
