Part 2, the spec .kiro/specs/vampire-fees/ (requirements with REQ-VF ids and acceptance examples with concrete input and exact output, design, test-first tasks VF1 and up). Vampire Fees finds the bank fees that drain an account, for individuals and small businesses, from a bank statement in CSV or Norma 43. A fee is a debit whose Norma 43 common concept is 17 (interest, fees and taxes) or 16 (stamp duty and brokerage), or whose counterparty or description matches a fee vocabulary kept in config.toml (accent and case insensitive, Spanish and English words such as comision, cuota de mantenimiento, descubierto, intereses deudores, gastos, fee, commission, overdraft, maintenance); state how the concept tag from the Norma 43 reader is used and how a CSV row without concept is classified. Detectors in namespace vf.: vf.recurring_fee (the same fee charged monthly, quarterly or yearly, with its yearly cost), vf.fee_increase (a recurring fee whose amount rises), vf.overdraft_cost (overdraft interest and overdraft fees over the statement), and one summary alert with the total fees of the statement and their yearly equivalent, citing every fee row. Severities and thresholds as named constants with reasons. Vampire theme on the page (a coffin card per fee with its yearly cost, dark red accent within the shared dark theme), the hallucinating switch and the number guard exactly as in the other apps, a demo format choice CSV or Norma 43 with the same alerts. Demo data synthetic and seeded with realistic noise and hard negatives that must not be fees (a shop whose name contains a fee word only by chance, a refund of a fee, a one-off notary fee that is not a bank fee if you decide so, with the reason). Constraint to measure the template claim: the app must need no change in ledger_kit or in the other apps; if the design finds that a library change is unavoidable, list it as an explicit decision instead of hiding it. Add a task that records, in the spec, how many Kiro calls and how much time the app took, filled in later by the reviewer.
