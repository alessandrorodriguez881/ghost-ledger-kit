A. Norma 43 bank statements (AEB Cuaderno 43, Serie normas y procedimientos bancarios n. 43, Informacion normalizada de cuenta corriente, June 2012). Fixed records of 80 characters, positions 1-based. Record 11 account header: 1-2 code 11, 3-6 bank code, 7-10 branch, 11-20 account number, 21-26 start date YYMMDD, 27-32 end date YYMMDD, 33 sign of the opening balance (1 debtor, 2 creditor), 34-47 opening balance (14 digits, 2 implied decimals, no comma), 48-50 currency as ISO 4217 numeric code, 51 information mode (1, 2 or 3), 52-77 short account holder name, 78-80 blank. Record 22 main movement (mandatory): 1-2 code 22, 3-6 blank, 7-10 originating branch, 11-16 operation date YYMMDD, 17-22 value date YYMMDD, 23-24 common concept code, 25-27 own concept code, 28 debit or credit key (1 debit, 2 credit), 29-42 amount (14 digits, 2 implied decimals), 43-52 document number, 53-64 reference 1, 65-80 reference 2. Record 23 complementary concept, optional, at most 5 after one 22, sequence 01 to 05 in positions 3-4: 5-42 concept text, 43-80 concept text. Annex 4 SEPA direct debits (mode 3) reuse record 23 with a fixed layout: 23-01 has 5-8 scheme CORE or B2B (AT-20) and 9-78 creditor name (AT-03); 23-02 has 5-39 creditor identifier (AT-02) and 40-74 unique mandate reference (AT-01); 23-03 has 5-8 purpose (AT-58), 9-12 purpose category (AT-59) and 13-80 remittance concept (AT-22); 23-04 has 5-76 concept continuation; 23-05 has 5-39 creditor reference (AT-10) and 40-80 debtor name. Record 24 optional currency equivalence, only when the original currency differs from the account currency: 3-4 code 01, 5-7 original currency numeric code, 8-21 amount in that currency (2 implied decimals). Record 33 end of account: 3-6 bank, 7-10 branch, 11-20 account, 21-25 number of debit entries, 26-39 total debits, 40-44 number of credit entries, 45-58 total credits, 59 sign of the closing balance (1 debtor, 2 creditor), 60-73 closing balance, 74-76 currency, 77-80 blank. Record 88 end of file: 3-20 eighteen nines, 21-26 number of records in the file excluding the 88 itself, 27-80 blank. Common concept codes from Annex 2: 01 cheques and withdrawals, 02 deposits, 03 direct debits and bills, 04 transfers, 05 loan repayments, 06 bill remittances, 07 subscriptions of securities, 08 dividends and coupons, 09 stock operations, 10 fuel cheques, 11 ATM, 12 credit and debit cards, 13 foreign operations, 14 returns and unpaid items, 15 payroll and social security, 16 stamp duty and brokerage, 17 interest, fees and taxes, 98 cancellations and corrections, 99 other. Annex 2 currency codes: 978 EUR, 840 USD, 826 GBP, 756 CHF, 752 SEK, 578 NOK, 554 NZD, 392 JPY, 208 DKK, 124 CAD, 036 AUD, and any other ISO 4217 numeric code.
Reader design to specify: parse_norma43 in ledger_kit/ingestion/norma43_reader.py with the same contract as parse_csv (list of Transaction and list of quarantine records); one Transaction per 22 record with date from the operation date, amount from the 14 digits divided by 100, flow from the key, currency from the account header, source_ref of the form file:line where line is the 1-based line of the 22 record; counterparty and description rules for a SEPA direct debit (creditor name, remittance concept) and for generic 23 records, and a fallback to the common concept label when there is no 23; creditor identifier, mandate reference, concept codes and the record 24 original currency kept in tags. Real files are messy: lines may lack trailing spaces, end in CRLF, be Latin-1 encoded, and contain several accounts. Specify validation and quarantine reasons (unknown record code, 22 before any 11, 23 without a 22, more than five 23, bad date, non-digit amount, unknown currency code) and the control checks that must quarantine a whole account block rather than silently accept it: record 33 counts and totals against the parsed movements, opening balance plus credits minus debits against the closing balance with the debtor and creditor signs, and the 88 record count. Years are 2000 plus YY.

Facturae e-invoices (official Spanish XML format; version 3.2.2 target namespace http://www.facturae.gob.es/formato/Versiones/Facturaev3_2_2.xml, root Facturae, also accept 3.2 and 3.2.1 by matching local element names and reading FileHeader/SchemaVersion). Paths from the root: Parties/SellerParty/TaxIdentification/TaxIdentificationNumber (PersonTypeCode F or J, ResidenceTypeCode E, R or U); SellerParty/LegalEntity/CorporateName or SellerParty/Individual/Name with its surnames; Invoices/Invoice/InvoiceHeader/InvoiceNumber and optional InvoiceSeriesCode; InvoiceIssueData/IssueDate (xs:date) and InvoiceCurrencyCode; TaxesOutputs/Tax with TaxTypeCode (01 is IVA), TaxRate, TaxableBase/TotalAmount, optional TaxAmount/TotalAmount, optional EquivalenceSurcharge and EquivalenceSurchargeAmount/TotalAmount; TaxesWithheld/Tax (04 is IRPF); InvoiceTotals/TotalGrossAmountBeforeTaxes, TotalTaxOutputs, TotalTaxesWithheld, InvoiceTotal and TotalOutstandingAmount; PaymentDetails/Installment/InstallmentDueDate, InstallmentAmount, PaymentMeans (04 transfer, 02 direct debit) and AccountToBeCredited/IBAN (or AccountNumber). Amounts are xs:double with up to eight decimals: read them as Decimal from the text, never through float, and define how they are rounded to cents. A file has one seller and may hold several invoices.
