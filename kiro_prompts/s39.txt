Sizes 212928, 229312 and 237504 bytes. xlrd 2.0.1 reads them; it prints a harmless warning about the file size not being a multiple of the sector size, which must not stop the import.
F2. F3. A block runs from its label column to the column before the next non-empty label; header columns with an empty concept inside a block are blank spacers. The blocks, in order: Proveedor de la Cuenta (col 6 Nombre de la cuenta); Mantenimiento de la cuenta (col 8 Periodicidad, col 10 Comisión ANUAL total (euros)); Emisión y mantenimiento de una tarjeta de débito (col 12 Marca comercial, col 15 Comisión de emisión (euros), col 16 Comisión mantenimiento. Columns must be found by header text, never by position.
F4. Data: from the row after the concept row to the last row, one row per account (declaration). Column 0 (entity code, four-digit text) and column 2 (entity name) are filled only on the first account of each entity; the next accounts of the same entity leave them empty and belong to the entity above. Column 5 is a running number 1 to n stored as a number cell (1.0, 2.0, ...); it is the only number cell. No merged cells. Up to 20 accounts per entity.
F5. Every value cell is text. Amounts use a decimal comma and two decimals (240,00; 0,00; 12,02); no thousands separator was seen (the highest is 300,00). A lone asterisk is the asterisk. Text cells carry trailing spaces only (up to about 200); no other padding character was seen.
F6. The two compared concepts are spelled differently from the screen: Comisión ANUAL total (euros) and Comisión mantenimiento. ANUAL total (euros), with ANUAL in capitals.
F7. The asterisk legend in the Excel is '(*) No se cobra comisión/no se ofrece el servicio' (no fee is charged, or the service is not offered); on screen it said No practicado.
F8. D7.
