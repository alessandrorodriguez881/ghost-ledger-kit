Five narrow changes, in ledger_kit/explainer.py only:
1. Row citations: a number is not a figure when it is the line number of one of the alert's own source refs and it is written as a row or line citation: right after row, rows, line or lines (any case; a list such as 181, 236, and 292 counts), or as an ordinal right before row or line (8th row). Every other number keeps today's rule: row 999 when 999 is not a cited line stays rejected, and 181 EUR stays rejected unless 181 is allowed today.
2. Count of cited rows: an integer equal to the number of the alert's source refs is allowed when the next word is one of rows, transactions, charges, payments, debits, entries, invoices, times (singular or plural, an optional adjective such as debit in between is fine). Every other count keeps today's rule.
3. List markers: a number of one or two digits at the start of a line, followed by a dot or a closing parenthesis and a space, is a list marker, not a figure. The same digits in the middle of a line keep today's rule.
4. Currency: when the cited rows or the alert data name at least one currency, a currency symbol or ISO code in the answer that is not among them rejects the answer, and that token is reported like a rejected number (euro sign for EUR, dollar sign for any dollar currency, pound sign for GBP, yen sign for JPY; codes are the ones ledger_kit/ingestion/iso4217.py knows).
