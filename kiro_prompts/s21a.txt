2. New module ledger_kit/number_words.py, standard library only. One public function finds the number phrases of a text and returns, for each one, the text exactly as written, its start and end offsets, its integer value and how many number words it has.
- English: zero to nineteen, the tens twenty to ninety, hundred, thousand, million. A ten and a unit join with a hyphen or a space (ninety-nine, ninety nine). The word and joins only right after hundred, thousand or million (five hundred and forty, two thousand and five). A leading a before hundred, thousand or million counts as one (a hundred).
- Spanish: cero, uno, una, un, dos to quince, dieciséis to diecinueve, veinte, veintiuno to veintinueve, treinta to noventa, cien, ciento, doscientos to novecientos and their feminine forms (doscientas and so on), mil, millón, millones. Accept the forms with and without accents (dieciséis and dieciseis, veintidós and veintidos, veintitrés, veintiséis). The word y joins only a ten and a unit (treinta y cinco). Examples: quinientos cuarenta, mil doscientos, un millón doscientos mil.
- Words join only where that grammar allows; anything else starts a new phrase (five and six gives two phrases, 5 and 6; el cinco gives one phrase, 5). The words and and y never start or end a phrase.
- Matching is case-insensitive and on whole words only. A phrase joined by a hyphen or a space to an ordinal word inside the same phrase (twenty-first, ninth, tenth, primero, segundo, vigésimo and the like) is not a number: skip the whole phrase, so the twenty-first of August gives nothing. one-off gives one phrase, one, value 1.

3. Which phrases the guard checks, inside _passes_number_guard, on the same text the digits are read from, after the digit check:
- A phrase is a figure to check when its value is above ten or it has two or more number words.
- A phrase of one word worth ten or less is checked only when the next word or the word before is a currency (euro, euros, eur, €, dollar, dollars, dólar, dólares, usd, $, pound, pounds, libra, libras, gbp, £, cent, cents, céntimo, céntimos) or a percent (percent, per cent, %, por ciento). So three months and one of them are not checked, five euros and cinco por ciento are.
- Exception: the single word once is checked only next to a currency or a percent, because it is also an English word (charged once).
- The value is checked against the same allowed set as digits (_allowed_decimals). Number phrases found in the alert data and in the visible row cells are read too, and their values join the allowed set, so a name such as SEVEN ELEVEN in a row never rejects itself.
- A rejected phrase is reported exactly as written (five hundred and forty) in rejected_numbers, like a digit token, in text order together with the digit tokens of the same text.
- Because this lives in _passes_number_guard, explain_with_meta, check_figures (the Exorcise drafts and scripts/ledger_scan.py check) and Night Watch all get it with no other change. Keep every existing rule for digits, dates, IBANs and currencies exactly as it is.

4. Highlighting: ledger_kit/live_guard.py highlight_segments must mark a rejected phrase in the raw answer as one segment. For a token made only of letters, spaces and hyphens, match whole words only (once must never be marked inside another word). Keep the digit behaviour as it is.
